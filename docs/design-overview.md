# Design Overview

This document outlines the design and architecture of Momentum.

## Overview

Momentum brings your to-dos, reminders, and CalDAV tasks into one flow—visible as lists or boards. Designed for people who want to see their life in motion.

There are many to-do list apps and there are also many Kanban apps. They all generally use proprietary backends to store the information about the tasks. Momentum uses CalDAV Tasks ("VTODO" as specified in IETF RFC 5545) as its backend. Momentum also supports Journal entries as a way for tracking project meetings and reports ("VJOURNAL"). Because Momentum uses CalDAV, it gives you flexibility in where you host your data.
